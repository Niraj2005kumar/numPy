31. OUTLIER DETECTION - IQR
============================================================

CODE:
Q1 = df["Salary"].quantile(0.25)
Q3 = df["Salary"].quantile(0.75)
IQR = Q3 - Q1

lower = Q1 - 1.5 * IQR
upper = Q3 + 1.5 * IQR

outliers = df[
    (df["Salary"] < lower) |
    (df["Salary"] > upper)
]

KYA:
IQR method se unusually low/high observations identify karta hai.

KYU:
Outliers statistics aur analysis ko strongly affect kar sakte hain.

IMPORTANT:
Har outlier error nahi hota.
Example:
Ek employee ki salary bahut high ho sakti hai aur wo actual value ho sakti hai.
Isliye outlier ko remove karne se pehle business context check karo.


REMOVE OUTLIERS:

CODE:
df = df[
    (df["Salary"] >= lower) &
    (df["Salary"] <= upper)
]

KYA:
IQR boundaries ke bahar ke records remove karta hai.

IMPORTANT:
Ye tabhi karo jab analysis/business context ke according removal justified ho.

57. HOW TO LEARN THESE CODES
============================================================

Sirf syntax memorize mat karo.

Har code ko ek business question se connect karo.

QUESTION:
Top 10 customers kaun hain?

CODE:
df.groupby("Customer")["Sales"].sum().nlargest(10)

LOGIC:
Customer -> Group
Sales -> Sum
nlargest(10) -> Top 10


QUESTION:
Department-wise average salary?

CODE:
df.groupby("Department")["Salary"].mean()

LOGIC:
Department -> Group
Salary -> Average


QUESTION:
Salary me missing values kitni hain?

CODE:
df["Salary"].isnull().sum()

LOGIC:
isnull() -> Missing identify
sum() -> Count


QUESTION:
Highest salary wale 10 employees?

CODE:
df.nlargest(10, "Salary")

LOGIC:
Salary -> Sort/compare
nlargest(10) -> Top 10
